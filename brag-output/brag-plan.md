# Brag Plan: Nuur

## What is this app?
A premium craft coffeehouse website in Amsterdam — 7 cinematic pages with GSAP scroll animations, a full cart system, and reservation flow. Pure ingredients, intentional preparation.

## The angle
Treat this like a luxury brand film. Not a tech product launch — a quiet, confident showcase of craft. The video should feel like stepping into an Amsterdam canal-side cafe at golden hour.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The NUUR wordmark scales in over the cinematic hero image — full-screen coffee close-up with dark gradient. One word. That's the hook.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The hero text reveals: "Pure Ingredients. Intentional Preparation." — gold accent on cream
- Menu product cards cascade in one by one — the Cappuccino, Avocado Toast, Almond Croissant
- The seasonal collection appears — "Autumn at Nuur" with warm-toned cards

## Outro / punchline
"Prinsengracht 42, Amsterdam" fades in below the logo. Quiet confidence. No call to action needed.

## User flow worth showing
none — landing-page only. Rely on the strongest visuals: hero, menu cards, seasonal collection.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium product film
- Interpretation: Slow reveals, generous holds, restrained motion. The product speaks for itself. No hype, no urgency — just craft.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #110d08 (brown-950)
- Accent: #f6d285 (gold-400)
- Text: #fdf8f3 (cream-50)
- Display font: Space Grotesk
- Body font: Inter
- Strongest visual element: The cinematic hero with dark gradient overlay and gold typography

## Share copy (draft)
Nuur. Pure ingredients. Intentional preparation. A craft coffeehouse on Prinsengracht.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3 (steady and clean, best for polished)
- Music treatment: fade in at 0.3s, low volume 0.3, sustain through, fade out at 18s
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; use music RMS to make the hero glow and product card presence breathe
- SFX posture: sparse — 2-3 subtle cues only
- Audio-coupled moments:
  - Scene 1 (hero) — soft reveal sound on wordmark entrance
  - Scene 2 (text) — gentle accent on gold text reveal
  - Scene 4 (outro) — quiet bell on final logo
- Restraint rule: no aggressive sounds. Every SFX must feel like it belongs in a quiet cafe.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hero Reveal — 5s
Full-screen coffee image with dark gradient. The NUUR wordmark scales in from 0.95 to 1.0 with a subtle glow. Clean, confident, no rush.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: quiet announcement — the brand arrives
Audio-coupled idea: soft reveal sound on wordmark entrance
Music: warm bed fades in
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Tagline — 5s
"Pure Ingredients." appears on line 1, then "Intentional Preparation." on line 2. Gold accent color. Each line holds long enough to read.
Sequential/interaction: yes — two lines appear sequentially
Audio intent: gentle emphasis on each line
Audio-coupled idea: subtle accent on gold text reveal
Music: steady, warm
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Menu Showcase — 6s
Three product cards cascade in one by one: Cappuccino (€4.25), Avocado Toast (€12.50), Almond Croissant (€5.50). Each card has a food image, name, and price. Cards are styled like the actual ProductCard component.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 3 cards arrive one by one
Audio intent: rhythmic, satisfying arrivals
Audio-coupled idea: card-by-card reveal with soft card sounds
Music: steady, building slightly
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Outro — 4s
NUUR wordmark centered. "Prinsengracht 42, Amsterdam" below. "Puur genieten" tagline fades in. Long hold. Quiet confidence.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: final landing — the brand settles
Audio-coupled idea: quiet bell on final logo
Music: fades out
Transition mood: fade to black
